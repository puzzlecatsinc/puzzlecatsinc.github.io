Find your next challenge.

Sudoku is a number-placement puzzle: fill a nine-by-nine grid so that each row, column and three-by-three box contains the digits one through nine without repetition. The numbers act as symbols, so progress comes from eliminating possibilities rather than performing arithmetic.

Choose Easy, Medium, Hard or Expert, then make the puzzle your own with candidate notes, hints and automatic checking. Daily challenges give you a fresh reason to return, and statistics help you follow your progress.

Play offline when you want a quiet number puzzle on hand. Whether you have a few minutes or time for a harder grid, choose the difficulty that fits your session.

Look for a reason before entering a digit

Start with a row, column or box that has relatively few empty cells. For each empty cell, consider which digits are excluded by its three units. Candidate notes can help keep that reasoning visible; they are most useful when you update them consistently after a confirmed placement.

Choose how much help you want

Use candidate notes to keep track of possibilities or turn to a hint when you want help taking the next step. Try a harder difficulty when you are ready for a longer challenge.
