Find your next challenge.

Solitaire Word Associations puts complete words on cards and asks you to recognize how they belong together. Category-heading cards form the foundations: place word cards under the appropriate category and work toward clearing the board. The central task is understanding relationships between words, rather than spelling a word from individual letter cards.

Plan within a move limit, earn stars for clearing boards and use hints or undo when you want help. Offline play lets you keep a word-card puzzle close at hand.

Follow the story and renovate a mansion as you progress. Solitaire Word Associations is published by Solitaire Games Free Inc.

Also available on Android as Solitaire Word: Card Puzzles.

Read the category before moving the word

A word may suggest more than one association in ordinary language. Inspect the category headings and the other visible cards before committing a move. When a move uncovers another card, the extra information may help you understand how the remaining groups fit together.

Word solitaire can mean different things

This game sorts complete word cards by category. A letter-connection puzzle such as Word Hunt instead asks you to construct words from letters. Other games may use the term differently again, so screenshots and a description of the actual move are more useful than the genre name alone.

Before you play.

Do I spell words from individual letter cards?

No. Place complete word cards into categories in this word-association card puzzle.

Is this traditional Spider or Klondike solitaire?

No. It uses cards and foundations, but the organizing rule is a word’s category rather than the suits and ranks of a standard playing-card deck.

Can I play offline?

Yes. The word-card puzzle supports offline play on iPhone and iPad.

Are hints and undo available?

Yes. Use hints and undo when you want help. See the app for current usage limits and offers.

Where can I download the game?

Choose the App Store for iPhone and iPad, or Google Play for Android. The Android listing is named Solitaire Word: Card Puzzles.
