Merge Clash: Tower Defense combines a five-tower deck with decisions made during a defensive battle. Choose towers with different abilities, develop your collection and decide when a merge is worth changing the board you already have. It belongs to the tower-defense side of merge games, rather than the falling-fruit or object-organization categories.

Build your collection of more than 50 towers and upgrade the ones that fit your strategy. Challenge rivals in PvP, defend together in co-op, or try Draft mode against bosses with different abilities.

Every merge asks you to adapt. Matching towers can turn into a different tower type, changing the roles available on your board and the choices for your next move.

Think about roles before a deck list

Read what each tower contributes to the deck and consider what happens when a merge removes a useful role from the board. A strategy should account for the towers and upgrades you actually own. There is no single deck recommendation here that assumes every player has the same collection.

Build a strategy around your collection

Choose towers whose abilities work together, then adapt as the battle changes. Use the upgrades and towers you own to find a deck that suits your play style.

Before you play.

Is Merge Clash a fruit-merging puzzle?

No. This app is a tower-defense game built around tower decks, merges, enemies and battle modes.

Does merging always keep the same tower type?

No. The public descriptions explain that matching merges can produce a different tower type. Plan for an outcome that changes the composition of the board.

Which modes can I play?

Take on PvP battles, cooperative defense and Draft mode.

How many towers can I collect?

Build your collection from more than 50 towers and upgrade the ones that fit your deck.
