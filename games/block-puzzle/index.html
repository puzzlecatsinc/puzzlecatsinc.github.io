Find your next challenge.

Block Puzzle turns a simple placement decision into a space-management challenge. Place shapes into open cells, complete lines to clear space and keep enough room for the next pieces. The iOS listing presents this core loop as a high-score game: each placement affects both the points available now and the spaces you will need later.

On Android, plan across an eight-by-eight grid with sets of three blocks. Clear multiple lines for bonuses and build a high score worth beating. Keep an eye on the shape of your open space as well as the number of empty cells.

Make room for shapes, not just points

Before placing the first piece in a set, consider where all the remaining shapes could fit. A narrow gap may count as empty space yet be unusable for the blocks you need to place. Completing a line can help, but leaving a flexible area for a large shape can sometimes be the more useful move.

Keep the whole set in mind

Before placing a block, look at all the shapes waiting for space. Flexible openings give you more choices as the board fills.

Before you play.

Is this the same as a falling-block game?

The listed core mechanic is placing supplied shapes into a grid and clearing completed lines. It should not be confused with a game where pieces continuously fall under a timer.

What can I track?

On Android, your saved high score gives you a target to beat in the next round.
