Find your next challenge.

Car Games combines driving exploration with missions and vehicle progression. The iPhone and iPad listing describes an online open world, a catalogue of more than 50 vehicles, missions that earn rewards and opportunities to buy and upgrade cars. This is a driving sandbox to explore, rather than a claim to reproduce a particular motorsport simulation.

Explore an online open world on iPhone and iPad, choose from more than 50 vehicles and earn rewards through missions. Buy and upgrade cars, take on city-boss races and drive with PlayStation or Xbox controllers on compatible devices.

On Android, take cars, buses and trucks into an open driving world. Tackle missions, ramps and races, or enjoy offline driving.

Before you play.

Can I use a controller?

On iPhone and iPad, Car Games supports PlayStation and Xbox controllers on compatible devices.

Can I play Car Games without internet?

Android offers an offline driving mode. On iPhone and iPad, explore the online open world. Online modes require a connection.

Are VIP options available?

On iOS, optional weekly, monthly and annual VIP offers provide additional benefits. See the App Store for current regional prices, renewal and cancellation terms.

Where can I find privacy information or help?

Read the Puzzle Cats privacy policy and app-specific store disclosures. For help, email contact@puzzlecats.com.
