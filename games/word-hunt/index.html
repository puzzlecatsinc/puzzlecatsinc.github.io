Find your next challenge.

Word Hunt is a letter-connection puzzle: swipe through the available letters to discover words and complete a board. The challenge comes from seeing another arrangement in a familiar group of letters, rather than sorting complete word cards into categories or racing another player to claim a grid.

Take on thousands of puzzles at your own pace on iPhone and iPad, without timers or a lives limit. Complete puzzles to earn in-game coins as you find new arrangements in familiar letters.

On Android, connect letters through more than 10,000 puzzles and enjoy changing scenery as you progress.

A different kind of word game

In a letter-connection puzzle you construct a word from individual letters. In a word-association card game, the words are already written and the task is to recognize their categories. Neither format is inherently better: choose spelling and pattern recognition, or category reasoning, according to the kind of puzzle you enjoy.

Build a useful solving habit

When a board stalls, try a different starting letter or look for common endings before repeating the same swipe. Changing the order in which you inspect the letters can reveal a word you overlooked.

Before you play.

Is Word Hunt a timed multiplayer word game?

This listing describes a level-based letter-connection puzzle. The iOS description explicitly says there are no timers or lives; it does not describe a head-to-head timed mode.

How many puzzles are there?

Enjoy thousands of puzzles on iPhone and iPad. The Android game offers more than 10,000 puzzles.

Are subscriptions available?

An optional weekly subscription is available on iOS in supported regions. See the App Store for the current offer and renewal terms.
