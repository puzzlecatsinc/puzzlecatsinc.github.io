Solitaire Explorer: Tripeaks brings the one-higher-or-one-lower rhythm of TriPeaks to a journey through scenic destinations. In the general TriPeaks ruleset, you remove exposed cards by matching a rank immediately above or below the active card. Revealing a covered card can open another route through the layout.

Set off through scenic destinations with readable cards and a journey map. Each stop brings another TriPeaks board and another chance to plan the chain of cards you want to uncover.

Consider what a move will uncover

When two exposed cards are both playable under a TriPeaks ruleset, compare the cards each move reveals. Opening a covered card can create a longer chain than removing an isolated card. You cannot know every hidden card in advance, so this is a way to examine choices rather than a promise of a winning sequence.

Learn the app’s rules before chasing a score

Before relying on a long sequence, check how the game handles aces and kings, the draw stock and any special cards. These details vary across TriPeaks games. Our strategy guide explains common decisions, while the app’s current instructions remain the authority for its exact rules and rewards.

Before you play.

Is this the same game as Spider Solitaire?

No. TriPeaks generally removes exposed cards by adjacent rank, while Spider builds complete suited sequences. They ask for different kinds of planning.

How do I progress?

Complete TriPeaks boards as you follow the journey map through scenic destinations.
